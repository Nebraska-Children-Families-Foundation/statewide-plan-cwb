# Managing Your Community Action Steps

Welcome to the Statewide Plan for Community Well-Being Portal! This guide will help you manage your Community Action Steps using our online system.

# Getting Started

## What is a Community Action Step?

A Community Action Step is a specific activity or initiative that your Community Collaborative is working on to improve well-being in your community. These action steps are part of Nebraska's larger Statewide Plan for Community Well-Being.

Each action step includes:
- A name that describes what you're doing
- Details about the activity
- Who is leading the work
- When you plan to complete it
- Current status of the work

## Logging Into the System

Before you can view or manage your action steps, you need to log into the portal:

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
2. Go to the portal website address provided by NCFF staff
3. Click the login button or link
4. Enter your email address and password
5. Click "Login" or "Sign In"

If you don't have login credentials or forgot your password, contact NCFF staff for help.

# Finding Your Action Steps

## Your Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see your Dashboard. This is your main page that shows:
- A summary of how many action steps you have
- The status of your action steps (Not Started, In Progress, Completed, etc.)
- A list of all your action steps in a table

The Dashboard is like your home base - you can always return here to see everything at a glance.

## My Community Activities Page

This page shows all the action steps that belong to your Community Collaborative. To get there:

1. Look for a link or menu item called "My Community Activities"
2. Click on it to see a detailed list

This page shows your action steps in a table format with columns for:
- Action Step Number (a unique identifier)
- Name of the action step
- Current status
- Who is leading the work
- Target completion date
- Action buttons (View, Edit, Delete)

## Viewing Action Step Details

To see more information about any action step:

1. Find the action step in your Dashboard or My Community Activities
2. Click the "View" button (usually looks like an eye icon)
3. You'll see a detailed page with all the information about that action step

The details page shows:
- Full description of the action step
- Current status and timeline
- Which Goal, Objective, and Strategy it connects to
- Who created it and when

# Creating New Action Steps

## Starting a New Action Step

To create a new action step for your collaborative:

1. Go to your Dashboard or My Community Activities page
2. Look for a button that says "Create New Action Step" or similar
3. Click the button to open the creation form

## Filling Out the Form

When creating a new action step, you'll need to provide:

### Required Information:
- **Activity Name**: A short, clear title for your action step
- **Activity Details**: A detailed description of what you plan to do
- **Community Collaborative**: Select your collaborative from the dropdown menu
- **Related Goal**: Choose which of the 4 main Goals this connects to
- **Related Objective**: Choose the specific Objective under your selected Goal
- **Related Strategy**: Choose the Strategy that best fits your action step
- **Activity Status**: Choose the current status (usually "Not Started" for new action steps)
- **Completion Year**: When do you expect to finish this work?
- **Completion Quarter**: Which quarter of the year (Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4)?

### Optional Information:
- **Activity Lead**: The name of the person leading this action step

## Selecting Goals, Objectives, and Strategies

The system uses dropdown menus that work together:
1. First select a Goal - this will show you the available Objectives
2. Select an Objective - this will show you the available Strategies
3. Select the Strategy that best matches your action step

Take your time to read through the options and choose the ones that best fit your work.

## Saving Your New Action Step

After filling out all the information:
1. Review everything to make sure it's correct
2. Click the "Save" or "Create" button
3. The system will assign a unique number to your action step
4. You'll be taken to the details page to see your newly created action step

# Editing Existing Action Steps

## When You Can Edit Action Steps

You can edit action steps that:
- You personally created
- Were created by other members of your Community Collaborative
- You cannot edit action steps from other Community Collaboratives

## Finding the Edit Button

Edit buttons appear in several places:
- On your Dashboard in the "Actions" column (looks like a pencil icon)
- On the My Community Activities page in the "Actions" column
- On the Action Step Details page (button says "Edit Action Step")

## Making Changes

To edit an action step:

1. Click any Edit button next to the action step you want to change
2. You'll see a form with the current information already filled in
3. Make your changes to any of the fields
4. The system won't let you change the Goal, Objective, or Strategy - contact NCFF staff if these need to change

### What You Can Change:
- Activity Name
- Activity Details
- Activity Lead
- Community Collaborative (if needed)
- Activity Status
- Completion Year
- Completion Quarter

### What You Cannot Change:
- Activity Number (automatically assigned)
- Plan Alignment (Goal, Objective, Strategy)

## Saving Your Changes

After making your edits:
1. Click "Save Changes" at the bottom of the form
2. The system will save your updates
3. You'll be taken back to the Action Step Details page
4. You'll see a green success message confirming your changes were saved
5. Review the details to make sure everything looks correct

## Canceling Changes

If you decide not to save your changes:
1. Click the "Cancel" button
2. You'll return to your Dashboard
3. None of your changes will be saved

# Understanding Action Step Status

## Status Options

Each action step has a status that shows how the work is progressing:

- **Not Started**: You haven't begun work on this action step yet
- **In Progress**: You're actively working on this action step
- **Completed**: You've finished this action step
- **Ongoing**: This is work that continues over time without a specific end date

## Updating Status

Remember to update your action step status as your work progresses:
1. Edit the action step
2. Change the "Activity Status" dropdown
3. Save your changes

This helps NCFF staff and other collaboratives see how community work is advancing across Nebraska.

# Tips for Success

## Writing Good Action Step Names

- Keep names short but descriptive
- Use clear, everyday language
- Avoid abbreviations others might not understand
- Examples: "Monthly Food Pantry", "Youth Leadership Program", "Senior Transportation Service"

## Writing Helpful Details

- Explain what you're doing in plain language
- Include who will benefit from this work
- Mention key activities or milestones
- Add context about why this work matters

## Setting Realistic Timelines

- Be honest about how long things take
- Consider factors that might cause delays
- It's better to set a longer timeline and finish early than to miss a deadline
- You can always update timelines if plans change

## Keeping Status Current

- Check your action steps monthly
- Update status when major progress is made
- Don't wait until something is 100% complete to mark it "In Progress"
- Use "Ongoing" for work that doesn't have a clear end date
